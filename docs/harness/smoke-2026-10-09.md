US-spelling text verified on screen: "When a window is maximized" checkbox (no clipping) and "when the taskbar is centered" in the About window.

New finding (pre-existing, upstream since 2022, `98d8ca8`): **clicking Apply moves keyboard focus from the settings window to the taskbar.** `AutoHide(false, …)` calls `SetWindowPos(taskbar, HWND_TOPMOST, …)` without `SWP_NOACTIVATE`, which activates `Shell_TrayWnd`. Keyboard users must click or Alt+Tab back after every Apply. Not a v3.7.2 regression. **Fixed in `31ec3ed`** (adds `SWP_NOACTIVATE` to both calls), not yet released. Verified on the CI build (run 38012396205, same exe hash, uncontained): after a mouse or keyboard Apply, with auto-hide off and on, the settings window keeps the foreground and focus stays on Apply; Alt+T works straight after; the taskbar stays shaped and topmost; the work area is normal after switching auto-hide back off.
